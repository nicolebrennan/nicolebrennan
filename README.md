# Hi, I am Nicole Brennan

I am an AI product manager and designer with more than 20 years in human-centered design. Pattern recognition is my superpower: I notice where people get stuck, I design the test that proves it, and I help the team ship the change that fixes it.

I think through products the way a chess player thinks through a game. I start with the end goal and work backward through every decision that gets us there.

## What I build

- **AI systems people can trust.** At Harvard Law School's Berkman Klein Center, I lead product and design for [NextSpace](https://nicole-brennan.com/nextspace), an open-source, AI-facilitated platform for live and hybrid events, and I built the [Personality Lab](https://nicole-brennan.com/personality-lab), a five-axis framework for measuring AI persona behavior.
- **Prototypes in code.** NextSpace, the Personality Lab, Reply Lab, and [Council of Elders](https://nicole-brennan.com/council-of-elders) all began as working prototypes. You can run an [open edition of Council of Elders](https://github.com/nicolebrennan/council-of-elders-open) yourself.
- **Identity people control.** I co-invented and designed [Keyring](https://nicole-brennan.com/keyring), an open-source decentralized identity wallet.
- **Public services for everyone.** For five years at GSA's Technology Transformation Services, I led UX for USA.gov, Vote.gov, and Search.gov, which serve more than 100 million people a year. I redesigned [USA.gov](https://nicole-brennan.com/usa-gov) and built its design system, and I pioneered the first federal [assistive technology research](https://nicole-brennan.com/assistive-technology-research) program.

## How I work

Accessible by default. Research with the people who use the product. Small, scrappy teams that move from a good question to a shipped product.
